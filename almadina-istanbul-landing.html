Our Story

A slice of Istanbul,
parked in Johar Town

Almadina Istanbul brings the Zia & Ziki kitchen to Civic Centre — thin-cut döner stacked high on the spit, dough stretched and fired the Turkish way, and sauces made fresh every morning, not bottled.

No pauses, no closing time. Whether it's a 2am craving or a Sunday family order, the grill stays lit.
